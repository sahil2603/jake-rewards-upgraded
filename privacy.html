PRIVACY POLICY

How JakeRewards handles your information when you use this website.

Last updated: June 2026

In short: JakeRewards is a lightweight community website. We do not require accounts, we do not collect personal financial information, and we do not sell your data. The leaderboard displays masked usernames provided by the affiliate platform.

1. Information We Collect

JakeRewards is designed to collect as little personal information as possible. We may process the following:

  • Leaderboard data: Usernames (partially masked for privacy) and wager statistics are pulled from the affiliate platform's reporting and displayed publicly on the leaderboard.
  • Technical data: Basic, anonymized analytics such as page views, browser type, and approximate region, used to understand traffic and improve the site.
  • Voluntary contact: If you reach out to us via Discord or other channels, we receive whatever information you choose to share.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect or store passwords, payment details, gambling account credentials, or sensitive personal identification. All gambling activity and account management happens on the third-party platform, governed by their own privacy policy.

3. How We Use Information

  • To display and update the public leaderboard and rewards.
  • To verify eligibility for promotions, giveaways, and referral rewards.
  • To understand and improve site performance through anonymized analytics.
  • To respond to inquiries you send us.

4. Username Masking

To protect player privacy, usernames shown on the public leaderboard are partially masked (for example, only the last portion of a username is shown). Full usernames are not published on the Website.

5. Cookies and Analytics

The Website may use basic cookies or third-party analytics services to measure traffic and improve performance. These do not personally identify you. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

6. Third-Party Links

This Website contains links to third-party platforms, including the gambling operator, Discord, and social media. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites. Please review their privacy policies separately.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Leaderboard statistics shared with us by the affiliate platform are displayed publicly in masked form as part of the service.

8. Children's Privacy

This Website is intended only for individuals of legal gambling age. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the legal age in their jurisdiction.

9. Your Choices

If you would like your masked username removed from the public leaderboard, or have any privacy-related request, please contact us via our Discord community and we will address it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, reach out via our Discord community.
